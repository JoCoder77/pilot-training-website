Programme Overview

Our Flight Training programme is the most direct, SACAA-compliant route from zero hours to a Commercial Pilot Licence with Instrument Rating. Designed around South Africa's unique training environment, the programme integrates ground school, simulator sessions, and flight hours into a seamless, accelerated structure.

You will fly the Cessna 172 — the world's most widely used training aircraft — under the instruction of active commercial airline captains. Every flight phase is linked to the next, eliminating the gaps that extend training timelines at other schools.

Whether you are a school leaver, a career changer, or an international student seeking SACAA licensing, this programme is designed for you.

Who Is This For?

  • School leavers pursuing an airline career
  • Career changers transitioning into aviation
  • PPL holders seeking CPL upgrade
  • International students seeking SACAA licence
  • Students from Africa, Middle East, Europe and beyond

What You'll Learn

  • Aircraft systems, airlaw, and meteorology (ground school)
  • VFR solo and cross-country navigation
  • Night flying currency and procedures
  • IFR procedures — holds, approaches, departures
  • Simulator training — partial and full panel
  • Crew Resource Management (CRM) fundamentals
  • SACAA written, oral, and flight test preparation
  • Human Factors and threat & error management
