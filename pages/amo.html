Programme Overview

Coral Sky Aviation operates as an Approved Maintenance Organisation (AMO) under SACAA certification, maintaining our own fleet of training aircraft. We are developing a structured programme to train the next generation of aviation maintenance engineers in this active operational environment.

Students will work alongside certified engineers on real aircraft — gaining the practical experience and theoretical knowledge required for aviation maintenance certification. The programme bridges the significant skills gap in African aviation maintenance.

Register your interest to be notified first when enrolments open.

Programme Scope

  • Airframe and powerplant fundamentals
  • Aviation regulations and SACAA compliance
  • Aircraft systems: hydraulic, electrical, avionics
  • Line maintenance procedures on training fleet
  • Technical documentation and record-keeping
  • Aviation Human Factors in maintenance
  • Workshop practice and tool management
