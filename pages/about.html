Our Story

Built on One Belief

Coral Sky Aviation was founded on the conviction that determined students, given the right training environment, can reach the right-hand seat of a commercial airliner. Over two decades, that belief has produced hundreds of licensed commercial pilots now flying across Africa, the Middle East, and beyond.

We operate from Rand Airport in Germiston — one of South Africa's most active general aviation hubs — giving our students unmatched exposure to real-world airspace from their first solo flight. Our fleet, simulator, and ground school are all on site. No commutes. No gaps.

Our SACAA approval is not a credential we display — it's a standard we maintain in every lesson, every exam, and every flight. When a Coral Sky graduate sits an airline selection test, they arrive over-prepared.

Based at Rand Airport

Rand Airport (FAGM) in Germiston, Gauteng is the oldest commercial airport in South Africa and one of the most active general aviation hubs on the continent. It provides exceptional training airspace — varied terrain, multiple navigation beacons, and close proximity to Johannesburg TMA for realistic IFR training.

Our facilities at Rand Airport include: a dedicated ground school classroom, state-of-the-art flight simulator bay, briefing rooms, student lounge, and direct ramp access to our training fleet.

Rand Airport is approximately 25km from Johannesburg CBD and 35km from Pretoria — easily accessible by road.
