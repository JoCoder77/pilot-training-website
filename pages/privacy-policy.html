Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 January 2026 · Last Updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Coral Sky Aviation (Pty) Ltd ("Coral Sky Aviation", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information submitted through our website and communications, in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and applicable South African privacy law.

1. Who We Are

Coral Sky Aviation (Pty) Ltd is a South African aviation training organisation registered under the Companies Act 71 of 2008 and approved by the South African Civil Aviation Authority (SACAA). We operate from Rand Airport, Germiston, Gauteng, South Africa.

Contact us regarding privacy matters: coralskyaviation@gmail.com

2. Personal Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Complete an enquiry form on our website
  • Contact us via email, WhatsApp or telephone
  • Apply for a programme or provide application documents
  • Subscribe to programme update notifications

This information may include your full name, email address, phone number, country of residence, nationality, date of birth (where required for application purposes), and any other information you voluntarily provide in correspondence.

We do not automatically collect personal information through cookies, analytics scripts, or tracking technologies without your knowledge.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information exclusively to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide programme information
  • Prepare and send personalised training quotations
  • Process and manage student applications and enrolments
  • Communicate important programme and training updates
  • Comply with SACAA and legal requirements where applicable

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing or commercial purposes.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers: Third-party services that help us operate our website (such as Formspree for form processing). These providers are contractually obligated to handle your data securely and in accordance with applicable law.
  • SACAA and regulatory bodies: Where required by law or SACAA regulation for student registration, medical reporting, or licence processing.
  • No other parties without your explicit consent.

5. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information for a maximum of 24 months. Student enrolment records are retained for the duration required by SACAA regulations and South African law. You may request deletion of your information at any time (subject to legal retention obligations) by contacting us.

6. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal obligations)
  • Object to the processing of your information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at coralskyaviation@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Information Regulator of South Africa: inforegulator.org.za

7. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Our website uses HTTPS encryption. Form submissions are processed through a third-party form provider with industry-standard security practices.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

8. Cookies

Our website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. If this changes, this policy will be updated and a cookie consent mechanism will be implemented.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites (such as WhatsApp). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
