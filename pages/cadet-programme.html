Programme Overview

The Cadet Pilot Programme is our most comprehensive offering — a structured, mentored pathway designed for students who want the full support structure of a cadet scheme combined with the speed and quality of Coral Sky's training environment.

Unlike a standard self-paced programme, the Cadet path includes airline industry mentorship, professional development workshops, simulator proficiency benchmarks, and active career placement support throughout training.

Cadets graduate not just with a licence — but with a portfolio, a network, and airline-ready CRM and simulator proficiency that sets them apart in selection centres.

Programme Highlights

  • Structured ab-initio from zero hours to CPL + IR
  • Active airline captain mentorship programme
  • Multi-Engine Rating included
  • CRM, human factors and SOP training
  • Airline interview and selection preparation
  • Career placement partnerships with regional carriers
  • Progress tracking and dedicated cadet coordinator

Entry Requirements

The Cadet Programme has slightly higher entry standards to ensure cadet cohort quality and airline-readiness.

RequirementDetail
Age18–30 years at commencement
AcademicMatric with Mathematics & Physical Science (minimum 60% each) or equivalent
MedicalSACAA Class 1 Medical — no waivers
EnglishICAO Level 4 minimum — testing available on request
AptitudePsychometric and aptitude assessment (arranged by Coral Sky)
InterviewPanel interview with training captain and programme coordinator
